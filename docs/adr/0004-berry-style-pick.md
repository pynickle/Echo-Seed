# Picking is berry-style

A Mature Echo Crop is Picked in place, like a sweet berry bush: the plant stays, leaves Mature, and must be grown again. It is not destroyed the way wheat is.

The player keeps a living plant as a fixture. Combined with range-only Presence this is an at-home fruit loop; drop tables must not also emit an Echo Seed on every Pick, or one crop becomes a seed factory.
