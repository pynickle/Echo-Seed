# Presence is range-only

The original brief required a player to *stay* (stand, sneak, or sleep) inside the range. We treat any player inside Presence Range as Present — walking, flying, swimming, and riding all count.

Stillness filters fight the stated goal (a plant that notices you are there) and are easy to get wrong (boats, beds, elytra). Range-only is the simpler rule; growth time must be long enough that walking past a farm does not instantly Mature every crop.
